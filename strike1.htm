Kenya Faces Major Transport Disruptions Amid Ongoing Matatu Strike

Stranded commuters waiting for transport during the strike.

Demonstrations and protests linked to transport issues.

Latest Updates

The ongoing matatu strike in Kenya has caused significant transport disruptions across major cities including Nairobi, Mombasa, and Kisumu. Thousands of commuters have been stranded as operators protest against new regulations and rising operational costs.

Students and workers are experiencing delays, while government officials continue negotiations with transport associations in an effort to restore normal operations.
